# Conditional rendering

`Show` renders its children while `when` is true, and its `fallback` otherwise:

```jsx
<Show when={temp() < 0} fallback={<p>No frost tonight.</p>}>
	<p class="warning">Frost warning!</p>
</Show>
```

When the condition flips, Solid creates the new branch and removes the old one, including any state inside it. A ternary works for small inline values; `Show` makes the branch a clear unit.

For more than two cases, `Switch` renders the first `Match` whose `when` is true, or its own `fallback`:

```jsx
<Switch fallback={<p>Cold</p>}>
	<Match when={temp() > 25}>
		<p>Hot</p>
	</Match>
	<Match when={temp() > 10}>
		<p>Mild</p>
	</Match>
</Switch>
```

Order matters: the first match wins.

**Try it:** replace the two nested ternaries with a `Show` and a `Switch`, then drag the slider through every case.
