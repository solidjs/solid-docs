`onCleanup` inside trackedEffect/onSettled.

## What you saw

- **Kind:** `lifecycle`
- **Severity:** `error` (broken behavior)

## Why it fires

`onCleanup` inside `createTrackedEffect` or `onSettled` is not supported.

## How to fix it

Return a cleanup function from the callback instead.
