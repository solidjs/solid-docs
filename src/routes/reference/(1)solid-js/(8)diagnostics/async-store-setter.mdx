Store setter callback returned a Promise (setters are synchronous transactions).

## Why it fires

A store setter callback returned a Promise — `setStore(async d => …)`, or a sync arrow whose helper is async.
A store setter is a synchronous transaction: the draft closes when the callback returns, so only the writes before the first `await` were in it; the rest are lost.

## How to fix it

Move the async orchestration into an `action()` and call the setter synchronously from inside it (`yield` to re-enter the transaction after an `await`).
Signals are not subject to this rule — a signal may hold a promise as its value.
